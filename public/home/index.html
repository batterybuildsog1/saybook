Questions

  • No. Nothing rewrites a story. The book lays out pages around their words, punctuation help is a suggestion they can skip, and the story helper answers questions without writing for them.

  • Pages form within a minute of a pause. Each picture takes several minutes to paint, sometimes ten or more when it’s busy, and kids keep writing while it does.

  • Only your family sees the books until a parent makes a link; anyone with that link can read it and could keep a copy. No ads, nothing sold. Audio from talking isn’t kept, and read-it-to-me recordings stay off unless you turn them on. Download or delete everything any time.

  • OpenAI and Anthropic models for the words and pictures, and ElevenLabs for reading aloud. The privacy notice says what each one receives.

  • Kids keep writing, and every book stays readable. To make new books, a parent picks one of the two plans, or runs their own copy.

  • Kids who tell stories: younger writers talk, older ones type. A parent or guardian holds the account, and kids don’t need a password.
