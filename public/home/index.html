What it does

Their words stay theirs

Nothing rewrites a story. The book lays out pages and chapters around their words. Punctuation help is a suggestion they accept or skip, and the story helper answers questions without writing for them.

Pictures that stay in character

Each character is drawn once and looks the same on every page. A kid’s own drawing can become the character.

Read aloud, word by word

A storyteller reads each page as it forms and lights each word as it’s read. Pages turn on their own. With a parent’s okay, kids can record their own reading instead.

Send it to friends

A parent makes a private link. Friends read the book and hear its pages, and nobody can change it. Stop sharing whenever you like.

Questions

Who is it for? Kids who love telling stories. Younger writers talk; older ones type. A parent or guardian sets it up and holds the account.

Does it write the story for them? No. Every word is theirs. The story helper answers when they ask, with feedback and labelled examples, never their story.

How long does a book take? Pages form a few seconds after a pause. Pictures take a few minutes, longer when it’s busy. They can keep writing, or come back later.

What does “about 8 books a month” mean? The plan includes a monthly amount of pictures and reading aloud, enough for about eight picture books. Writing always works, even when it’s used up.

Who can see the books? Only your family, until a parent makes a link for a book. Anyone with that link can read it and could keep a copy. You can stop sharing any time.

Is my child’s voice recorded? Talking to write turns speech into text, and we don’t keep the audio. “Read it to me” recordings are kept only if you turn them on.

Which AI does it use? Language and image models from established providers, and ElevenLabs for reading aloud. Self-hosted copies use whichever providers you choose.

Can I run it myself? Yes. It’s free and open source. Deploy it to your own Cloudflare account with your own keys; one OpenAI key is enough.

Why does it need a payment to start? The first payment also confirms you’re the parent or guardian. There’s no separate charge for that.

How do I cancel? From your account page, any time. Your books stay yours to download.
