Saybook terms

Saybook is operated and sold by HVAC Calc LLC. The account holder must be an adult parent or legal guardian. Children use writer profiles inside that account, not their own logins. Keep the parent password and recovery code private.

The subscription

The hosted plan costs US $19.50 each month and renews until canceled. Any applicable tax is shown at checkout. Stripe handles payment. Your first paid checkout also verifies the parent’s consent described in the parent notice; there is no separate consent charge. A free trial or unpaid checkout does not enable child use.

The initial allowance supports about 8 typical books per month; long books and redraws use more. Pictures can take a few minutes to paint; under busy shared load the first picture may take around 6–11 minutes. The initial monthly allowance is 120 image attempts, 1,000,000 text/vision tokens, and 50,000 newly generated narration characters per family. Portraits, redraws and unsuccessful image attempts count. Replaying a saved recording does not use new narration allowance. Allowances reset on the first of each month in UTC and do not roll over. The first month is not prorated for usage. The provider-dollar reserve is $2.25 per month. Parent settings shows consumption and held capacity. Enrollment starts with ten paid families; at capacity the waitlist takes only an adult email, with no charge or child data.

This is a shared-capacity service. Short-term rate limits, global daily limits, provider availability and maintenance can pause generation before a family uses its whole monthly allowance. The app keeps saved stories available and gives a gentle pause message. We do not promise unlimited generation or uninterrupted availability.

You can cancel renewal in Parent settings or the billing portal. Paid access continues until the current paid period ends unless you delete the account. Account deletion ends the subscription immediately and removes saved books and files. Export first if you want to keep a copy. The $19.50 charge is non-refundable except where the law requires. Contact support@saybook.app about mistakes or double charges; we review them case by case. See the cancellation and refund policy. Nothing here limits rights required by law.

Your stories and responsible use

Your family keeps its rights in the content it provides. You give us permission to store, process and display it, and send the necessary content to the service providers listed in the privacy notice, so the requested features work. You must have permission to upload pictures and recordings. Do not upload private information about other people or use the app for harassment, exploitation or harmful sexual or violent content involving children.

AI pictures and suggestions can be wrong or unsuitable. A parent should supervise use and review anything shared. The app is a writing tool, not a teacher, therapist or emergency service. Generated content is not guaranteed to be unique or copyrightable.

Sharing and removal

Books start private. A parent may separately authorize a revocable viewing link. Recipients may copy or forward what you share. You are responsible for checking that a shared book contains no details you want kept private. We do not contact friends or maintain a public gallery of children.

We may pause abusive use or a feature that a provider cannot supply. The current service uses subscription-backed AI access and ElevenLabs; those providers may change or suspend access. Their child-use and resale terms remain subject to the provider gaps disclosed in our security summary.

Books are removed at account deletion or after 365 days without authenticated account activity, as explained in the privacy notice. The free open-source version has separate hosting and provider costs chosen by its operator.
