Cancellation and refunds

Saybook is sold by HVAC Calc LLC. Each family’s first 5 books are free. After that, a plan costs US $19.50 per month or US $140.40 per year, and renews until canceled.

Cancel anytime

Open your Account page, then Manage billing, to cancel renewal anytime. Access runs to the end of the current paid period (the month, or the year on the yearly plan), subject to the ordinary usage and safety limits. Cancellation does not itself delete saved books.

Refunds and billing mistakes

Monthly ($19.50) and yearly ($140.40) charges are non-refundable except where the law requires. Canceling a yearly plan stops the next renewal; it does not refund the rest of the year. Switching from monthly to yearly credits the unused part of the month toward the yearly charge. If you were charged by mistake or charged twice, email support@saybook.app. We review mistakes and duplicate charges case by case. Nothing here limits rights required by law.

Deleting an account

Account deletion immediately cancels the subscription and removes saved books and files through our deletion process. Export first if you want a copy. Deletion does not automatically issue a refund or erase Stripe’s financial records.
