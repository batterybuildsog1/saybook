A note to parents and guardians

HVAC Calc LLC operates Saybook. Before your child uses it, please read this notice and the privacy notice. The parent signup checkbox records your agreement. Your first completed, paid $19.50 subscription checkout verifies that agreement using the card transaction. No child writing, recording, profile or upload is allowed until both steps are complete.

What you are agreeing to

For the story features your family requests, we collect and save writer nicknames, story words, uploaded drawings or reference pictures, generated illustrations, generated narration and usage records. We use them to save books, arrange their pages, make pictures, give requested writing help, and read them aloud. Your child’s draft words stay theirs; AI suggestions are optional.

Cloudflare hosts and stores the books and transcribes dictation. Our AI gateway passes requested text, vision and picture work to Anthropic/OpenAI services. ElevenLabs gets the text requested for generated narration with stock adult narrator voices. Stripe handles your subscription and notices about payment. Browser live speech recognition can involve the browser vendor’s speech service. These are the necessary recipients for the features you choose. Current upstream retention/training/security assurances are not fully verified; see the privacy notice. We do not claim every provider has approved this child-directed service.

Dictation audio is used for transcription and is not saved in our media storage. Your child’s original voice reading is saved only if you separately enable “Read it to me” recordings in Parent settings. It starts off. We do not clone children’s voices. Please do not put addresses, school names, phone numbers or other private details into stories, pictures or recordings.

Optional disclosure stays optional

You can consent to private story creation without consenting to a public share. A public viewing link needs a separate parent action and disclosure consent. Anyone with the link can view or copy the shared book. We do not sell data, show ads or build advertising profiles. We do not collect your child’s friends’ email addresses.

Optional usage analytics has a separate, unchecked parent choice. If you opt in, PostHog receives milestones linked to a hashed adult account ID, with categories and counts, after verified consent for child-related milestones. It receives no names, story words or media. Only parent pages may have masked recordings; child screens and shared books have none. You can turn optional analytics off in the page footer. See the privacy notice for details.

Consent and payment

By checking the parent consent box and paying, you affirm that you are an adult parent or legal guardian and authorize the collection and use described here. The payment is the ordinary $19.50 monthly subscription, not an extra verification fee. Checkout and the invoice notice identify the consent. The card or payment system must notify its primary account holder of the transaction. An abandoned or unpaid checkout, a free trial, or a saved card does not unlock the child features.

You may review or export the data, disable future saved recordings, cancel renewal, revoke share links, and delete the account in Parent settings. Deletion withdraws this consent and stops future collection. Sensitive changes ask for the parent password. You do not have to provide more child information than the requested features need.

Accounts that do not finish paid consent are removed after 7 days. Saved books are otherwise kept until deletion or 365 days without authenticated account activity. See the privacy notice for scheduled cleanup and provider/financial retention limits.
