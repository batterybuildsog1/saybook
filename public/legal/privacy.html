What we collect and why

When paid-family places are full, the waitlist keeps only an adult email for up to 30 days, without a payment or child account. For parents, we keep an email address, a protected password, consent records, subscription references, security sessions and usage totals. Stripe handles payment information. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code.

Inside your account we store writer nicknames, the stories your family chooses to save, uploaded drawings or reference pictures, generated illustrations and narration, and the settings needed to show and read your books. Please use nicknames and avoid putting addresses, school names, contact information or other private facts into stories and pictures.

Dictation audio is used for transcription, then discarded from the app’s request handling. It is not stored in our media bucket. The optional “Read it to me” feature is different: when a parent enables it, your child’s own reading is saved so your family can play it again. That permission starts off. We do not clone children’s voices.

Services that help run Saybook

Cloudflare hosts the app, database, files, queues and transcription. Text, vision and image requests currently pass through our privately operated AI gateway to upstream services from Anthropic and OpenAI. ElevenLabs receives the text requested for generated read-aloud and returns narration using stock narrator voices. Stripe manages the parent’s subscription, payment notices and billing portal.

These services receive the content needed for the feature you request. The current AI gateway uses subscription-backed services. We have not verified zero retention, no training, or child-specific contractual assurances across that route, and we do not promise them. Their terms and data practices apply to their processing. A browser’s live speech-recognition feature may also send speech to the browser vendor’s speech service; the final app transcription uses Cloudflare. Typing works without using a microphone.

No advertising or data sales

We do not show ads, sell personal information, or use children’s stories to build advertising profiles. Child writing and reading pages have no session replay or marketing tracking. Essential sign-in cookies and short-lived security counters keep accounts working and help prevent abuse. Production product analytics and session replay are off. Any future analytics change requires review and an updated notice as applicable; this notice does not authorize recording a child’s story for replay.

Private books and optional sharing

Books are private to the account by default. Creating a public share requires a separate parent choice and disclosure consent. Anyone with a share link can view what you chose to share and can make a copy. Turning off the link stops future access through Saybook, but cannot erase copies someone already made. We do not collect children’s friends’ emails or send invitations.

Your choices

A parent can review saved books, export account data and files, turn saved recordings off, cancel the subscription, revoke a share link, or delete the whole account. Sensitive controls ask for the parent password again. Deleting the account withdraws consent, stops future collection, cancels the subscription and removes its database rows and stored files. Existing work is locked while deletion completes; if a picture is still finishing, deletion may ask you to retry.

Canceling a subscription stops renewal and paid features when the paid period ends; it does not itself delete saved books. Turning recordings off prevents new saved readings. Use story deletion or account deletion to remove recordings already saved.

How long data stays

We keep saved books and media for the family’s use until the parent deletes them or the account is closed. We delete accounts after 365 days without authenticated account activity. Unverified parent accounts that never finish consent are removed after 7 days. Routine operational events expire after 30 days, sessions after 30 days, and account usage records after 396 days or sooner with account deletion. Security attempt counters expire after an hour. Cleanup normally follows at the next daily run; failures or work still finishing can delay completion and need a retry.

Cloudflare may keep recovery copies or infrastructure logs under its service settings. Stripe may retain invoices and transaction records for financial and legal obligations. Upstream AI retention and deletion are not fully verified. Exact account-specific provider, recovery-copy, queue and infrastructure-log expiry has not been verified. We do not promise immediate erasure from every provider or backup. See our retention schedule for the enforced deadlines and known limits, including browser caches and support mail, and our security program summary for safeguards and assurance gaps.
