How we protect family information

Alan Knudson at HVAC Calc LLC is responsible for Saybook’s written information security program. The program covers risks to children’s and parents’ information, safeguards, provider oversight, incident handling and at least annual review. The initial assessment is dated November 2, 2026; the first annual review is due by November 2, 2027.

Controls in the service

Children cannot use story, recording or upload features before the parent notice, affirmative consent and verified paid Checkout. Books start private. Family database queries and files are separated by account. Public sharing needs a separate parent choice and consent. Sensitive controls ask for the parent password again.

Passwords are protected with salted password hashing and a separate server secret. We store hashes of session tokens and recovery codes. Sign-in cookies use secure browser protections, sessions expire, and sign-in attempts are limited. Provider keys stay on the server. Account deletion locks the account and removes its saved files and database rows; delayed painting work checks that an account and story still exist.

Generation has account and global limits and can be paused. Optional saved original readings start off; we do not clone children’s voices. Production product analytics and session replay are off. Operational logging is enabled and can include job identifiers and errors; it is not the same as recording a child’s screen.

Providers and limits of our assurances

Cloudflare hosts the app, files, database, queue and transcription. Stripe processes subscriptions and payments. Our privately operated subscription-backed AI gateway sends text/vision work to Anthropic and pictures to OpenAI. ElevenLabs receives requested story text for stock-voice narration. Browser live speech recognition may involve the browser vendor. Support mail is forwarded to the operator’s mail systems.

We do not have a verified zero-retention agreement with Anthropic or OpenAI for the gateway route, or with ElevenLabs. No-training and child-specific written security assurances across that route have not been verified. ElevenLabs written approval for this child-directed app has not been obtained. Account-specific security assurances and recovery/log expiry for the hosting and mail systems have not been fully verified. We do not present those gaps as completed safeguards or provider approval.

Review and reports

The coordinator is responsible for reviewing risks, safeguards, incidents, deletion failures and providers at least annually, and after material changes. The app’s synthetic checks cover consent, family isolation, export, sharing, billing and deletion. This is not an independent security audit or a compliance certification. Report a concern privately to support@saybook.app; please do not send passwords, recovery codes, full card details or unnecessary child content.
