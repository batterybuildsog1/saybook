## Responsibility and scope

Alan coordinates access, provider selection, risk assessment, incident response and review. Changes to data collection, providers, sharing, authentication, retention or deployment require updating this assessment and the parent/privacy notices as applicable before enabling the changed processing. The service is limited to US enrollment, ten paid families initially. Paid enrollment remains closed until the release gates have evidence.

The inventory includes parent email and authentication/recovery hashes; versioned consent/payment references; writer nicknames and settings; stories, illustrations, uploaded references, saved original readings and generated narration/alignment; account-linked usage, operational errors and security counters. Dictation is processed transiently, not written to the app's R2 bucket. Browser live speech recognition can independently involve the browser vendor. Stripe holds payment details; the Worker does not store full card numbers or CVCs.

## Operating procedures

These are coordinator duties established by this program, not claims of already-running automations:

- Restrict production/provider administration to authorized operators. Keep keys out of source, browser assets, screenshots and support replies; use scoped credentials and secret bindings. Revoke exposed credentials and assess affected data. Do not rotate the auth pepper without a recovery migration.
- Review retention failures, deletion requests, billing/webhook errors and anomalous usage during operation. A failed deletion stays locked/retryable; investigate and complete it rather than claiming success. No independent alerting service or scheduled human-review automation is installed by this document.
- Run the deployed synthetic E2E for consent, cross-tenant access, sharing, billing/cancellation, export, media deletion and retention after relevant changes. Retain sanitized results; disable fixture controls/remove the test token and restore LIVE Stripe before handoff. Test cards only. Never use a child's book as a fixture.
- For a suspected incident: Alan triages scope, pauses affected generation or closes enrollment, revokes affected access/links as appropriate, preserves minimal restricted evidence, identifies vendor involvement, fixes and verifies the cause, and determines legally required parent/regulator notices and timing. Do not place child data in public issues. Record actions and follow-up; do not claim an untested recovery capability.
